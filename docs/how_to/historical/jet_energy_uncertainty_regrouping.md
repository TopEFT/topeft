# Historical TOP-22-006 JES uncertainty regrouping

> **Archival one-time maintenance note.** `topeft/modules/parsejec.py` combined
> JES uncertainties in quadrature for the final TOP-22-006 categories. It is
> not a current TOP-26-006 production entry point.

The historical inputs were the JetMET POG `RegroupedV2_Summer19UL*` files. The
resulting `Quad_Summer19UL1*` payloads were saved in the then-current shared JEC
data area. The present repository does not establish a maintained command,
current payload destination, or need to regenerate them. Treat any payload
regeneration or installation as a separate correction-payload decision.
